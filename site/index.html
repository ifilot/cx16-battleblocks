CLASSIC & CYBERPUNK

Pick your look.

Bright boards and colorful blocks, or neon against a dark circuit-board backdrop. Both styles include every game mode. Start in Classic, then press T in the game menu or tap Theme to switch.

HOW TO PLAY

A little friendly sabotage.

01 / CLEAR & ATTACK

Make room. Make trouble.

Complete rows to clear them. Clearing two, three, or four rows sends one, two, or four garbage rows to your opponent, arriving with their next piece. Your level rises every ten cleared rows.

02 / TAKE CONTROL

Every piece counts.

Player 1: ← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop. Player 2: A D move, W rotate, S soft drop, Q hard drop.

03 / PICK YOUR BATTLE

Play. Or just watch.

Choose solo, versus computer, local two-player, or computer demo. In the menu, T swaps Classic and Cyberpunk themes. Esc returns to the menu.
