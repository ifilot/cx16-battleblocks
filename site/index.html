HOW TO PLAY

A little friendly sabotage.

01 / CLEAR & ATTACK

Make room. Make trouble.

Complete rows to clear them. Clearing two, three, or four rows sends one, two, or four garbage rows to your opponent, arriving with their next piece. Your level rises every ten cleared rows.

02 / TAKE CONTROL

Every piece counts.

Player 1: ← → move, ↑ rotate, ↓ soft drop, Space hard drop. Player 2: A D move, W rotate, S soft drop, Q hard drop. Controllers: D-pad moves, B/Y rotates, A/X hard-drops, and Start/Select returns to the menu. Either controller can navigate menus.

03 / PICK YOUR BATTLE

Play. Or just watch.

Choose solo, versus computer, local two-player, or computer demo. In the menu, T cycles Commander X16, Cyberpunk, Foundry, Synthwave, and Matrix themes. Esc returns to the menu.
